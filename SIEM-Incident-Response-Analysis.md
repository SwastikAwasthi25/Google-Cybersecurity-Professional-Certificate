# Case Study: Leveraging SIEM & Logs for Business Resilience
**Author:** Swastik Awasthi  
**Focus:** Incident Response, SIEM Analytics, and Business Continuity  

## 🔍 Scenario Overview
In this analysis, I explored a critical business incident where a main e-commerce website suddenly went offline. The objective was to define how cybersecurity operations protect business continuity and identify the technical logs required for investigation.

## 🛡️ Cybersecurity Business Operations
Cybersecurity business operations encompass the processes that ensure the **security, availability, and integrity** of systems critical to business functions. 

### Key Impact Areas:
* **Financial Loss:** Direct impact on revenue due to website downtime.
* **Identity Theft:** Potential compromise of customer PII (Personally Identifiable Information).
* **Reputation Damage:** Loss of customer trust and brand value.

## 📋 Security Principles in Action
To prevent or respond to such outages, the following principles are applied:
* **Defense in Depth:** Layered security to ensure that if one control fails, others protect the asset.
* **Least Privilege:** Ensuring users only have access to what is necessary for their role.
* **Attack Surface Minimization:** Reducing the number of entry points for threat actors.



## 🛠️ The Role of Logs & SIEM
Detailed logs act as the "Source of Truth," while SIEM tools provide "Actionable Intelligence."

### 1. Critical Log Types
* **Network Logs:** Records of devices entering/leaving the network and connections between them. Crucial for identifying threat actor movements.
* **Server Logs:** Records of events related to services like logins, websites, and file shares. Essential for understanding specific service failures.

### 2. SIEM Functionality
A SIEM (Security Information and Event Management) tool centralizes and analyzes log data to:
* **Monitor Critical Activities:** Real-time tracking of organizational health.
* **Visualize Data:** Using metrics and dashboards to highlight suspicious patterns and high-risk events.
* **Prioritize Risks:** Correlating events to alert security teams before a breach escalates.



## 🎯 Conclusion: The Symbiotic Relationship
Logs provide the **evidence**, and the SIEM provides the **insight**. Together, they enable early threat detection, faster incident response, and operational resilience. This combination is critical for protecting business revenue and maintaining customer confidence in an e-commerce environment.
